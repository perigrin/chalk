- **Textbook-clean decomposition.** Every code issue landed in exactly ONE

  (0b0f6eda). i3 was filing-only (3 parked pilot issues, zero code) and stayed
  that way — the scope fence held with no leakage into buildable code. The
  double-assessor pattern (crochet:assess + ponytail, twice each) that produced
  this plan sized the issues right.
- **RED-first paid off.** i0 added corpus cases that failed on head before the
  fixes existed (auto-worklisted, floor untouched), so i1 and i2 each had a
  red->green oracle. The worklist mechanism behaved exactly as designed: a red
  non-GAP case became TODO without lowering the gate-green floor.
- **The mandatory self-verified gate caught a false completion — again.** The
  coordinator (not a subagent self-report) ran the FULL triple contract on
  committed HEAD and saw gate-green=152, not the plan's claimed 153/floor+1.
  This is the SECOND catch this session (the first: ir-node-unification i3
  hiding a 148->106 regression as reclassified-worklist). The gate is the
  single most load-bearing process control in this workstream.
- **Root cause over symptom.** The i32 truncation was diagnosed as a printf

  `%d`->`%lld` class across three sites, not the coercion refactor the first

  is visible in the shape of the diff.

## What Didn't Work

- **The plan's step-3 measurement was WRONG.** It claimed the divide fix gives
  "floor +1 / gate 148->150+". That was a BEHAVIOR-ONLY measurement. The real
  triple-contract result is gate-green=152, because the TypedInvariant leg stays
  RED: `Divide` requires its inputs to be Num OR bridged by an explicit Coerce
  node (TypedInvariant.pm:22, positively pinned by well-typed-graph.t
  H2-6/H2-7), and B::SoN emits `Divide(Int,Int)` stamped Num with NO Coerce
  node. The assessment measured the fix "regression-free at 148/worklist=0" but
  did not measure that it would *reach* the floor bump the issue mandated.
- **The floor-bump AC was unsatisfiable as written.** i2's original AC said
  "RAISE THE FLOOR (bump cmp_ok 148)". That bump was impossible without the
  fenced Coerce-node insertion. The issue could not honestly close on its own
  terms — it had to be re-scoped mid-execution (land backend fix, defer the
  floor-raise to the pilot). A correctly-measured plan would have written the
  AC as "behavior+shape green, invariant leg filed" from the start.

## What Puzzles Us

- Nothing anomalous in telemetry — the milestone was small and linear. The one
  genuine surprise was epistemic, not operational: a bug-fix that greens the
  BEHAVIOR leg can still leave the IR ILL-TYPED. The backend now lowers
  `Divide(Int,Int)` to correct output, yet the graph is invalid until a real
  Coerce node exists. "It produces the right answer" and "the IR is well-typed"
  are independent properties, and only the second is the invariant leg's job.
  This re-proved the session's central thesis from a new angle: coercion belongs
  in the IR AS A NODE, not implied by a stamp.

## What Will We Change

- **Add a third adversarial assessor lens: "did you measure ALL THREE legs?"**
  crochet:assess + ponytail are a value/correctness pair; neither is chartered
  to check that a claimed gate number was measured against the FULL contract
  (behavior AND shape AND invariant), not just the leg the fix obviously
  touches. For any plan whose success metric is the corpus triple-contract gate,
  a pre-execution lens must re-run the claimed number against all three legs on
  a probe branch. This exact flaw would have been caught pre-execution.
- **Floor-bump ACs must name the leg that gates the floor.** An AC that says
  "raise the floor" is only satisfiable if the change greens the leg currently
  holding gate-green down. Decomposer should require floor-raising issues to
  state WHICH leg they green (and confirm no OTHER leg still blocks) — else the
  AC is unsatisfiable and forces mid-execution re-scoping.
- **"The plan said X" is not evidence.** Re-measure the full contract on
  committed HEAD before accepting any gate claim. The self-verified gate already
  enforces this at close; the change is to distrust plan-stated numbers at
  refinement time too, not only at completion.
